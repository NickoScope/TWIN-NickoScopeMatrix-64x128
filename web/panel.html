Each LED's light is summed from the HUB75 stream the firmware drives out through LCD_CAM, over whole scan frames: 1.0 means the LED was lit for all of its row slot at 1/32 scan. The epoxy layer's glow (GOB) is off by default: its strength and radius are eyeballed, not yet matched to photos of the panel. The “GOB glow” slider turns it on.

    Everywherethe knob, the remote and the board's buttons on any screen

    IR remote

    Click for one press; hold and NEC repeats follow. The firmware itself decodes them from the receiver on GPIO0, as on the panel.

    Encoder

    Mouse wheel or drag turns it one detent at a time; it never presses the knob's switch, just like a real EC11. A click without dragging is a press; the firmware counts a long press as a normal one anyway.

    Room · SHTC3

    The air at the board's sensor (I2C0 0x70). The firmware reads it for the weather clock and smooths it over a minute.
